# Duel phases

A duel turn flows through phases: Draw -> Standby -> Main 1 -> Battle -> Main 2 -> End.
- Draw: turn player draws (first turn skips draw under Master Rule >= 3).
- Standby: optional trigger effects resolve here.
- Main 1: summon/set/activate, then either Battle or End.
- Battle: declare attacks. Battle Start -> Battle Step -> Damage Step.
- Main 2: leftover actions after battle.
- End: optional triggers, hand-size discard to 6, turn passes.

In ygocli prompts, "Go to Battle Phase" / "Go to End Phase" advance the phase.
