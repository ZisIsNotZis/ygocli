# Battle & damage

- Attack a monster: compare ATK. Attacker > defender ATK -> defender destroyed, difference is damage.
- Equal ATK: both destroyed, no damage.
- Attacker < defender: attacker destroyed, difference is damage to the attacker.
- Direct attack: no monsters on their field -> full ATK as damage.
- LP reaching 0 ends the duel (win by LP). Deck-out also loses (reason 2).
